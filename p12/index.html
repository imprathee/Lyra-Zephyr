Same-day delivery

Fast, Safe, Autonomous Delivery

LyraZephyr Aerial Logistics delivers medical supplies, food and parcels by drone, skipping traffic and cutting delivery times from hours to minutes.

Our Services

Medical Delivery

Urgent medicines and lab samples delivered between hospitals and clinics.

Available 24/7. Temperature-controlled boxes keep medicines safe.

Food Delivery

Hot meals and groceries delivered fresh to your doorstep.

Insulated bags. Meals arrive hot, usually within 30 minutes.

Parcel Delivery

Same-day delivery for documents and small packages.

Up to 5 kg. Every parcel can be tracked in real time.

How It Works

  1. Book your delivery online.
  2. A drone collects your package.
  3. Track the flight in real time.
  4. Receive your package at the drop point.
